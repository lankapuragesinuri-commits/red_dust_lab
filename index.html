01 THE RESEARCH QUESTION

A SMALL PARTICLE. A BIG ENGINEERING QUESTION.

Powering exploration
on a dusty world.

Solar energy is a valuable power source for robotic exploration. On Mars, airborne dust and surface deposition can reduce the light reaching solar-cell surfaces. This study uses a controlled, Earth-based experimental analogue to investigate the relationship between dust coverage and photovoltaic electrical output.

How does increasing surface coverage by a defined dust analogue affect photovoltaic output, and how effectively can selected mitigation methods restore performance?

RESEARCH NOTE

This project investigates selected aspects of the problem under controlled laboratory conditions. It does not reproduce the full Martian atmosphere or claim direct equivalence to performance on Mars.
